Crispy cornflake chicken coated in a hot honey glaze tucked in a wrap with lettuce, gooey cheese and with a chilli mayo dressing, this was an absolute BANGER 🤭

Ingredients (serves 2):
200g chicken breast
Salt
Pepper
Tsp garlic granules
Tsp smoked paprika
40g egg white
30g crushed cornflakes
40g lighter mayo
½ tsp chilli puree
20g honey
Tsp hot sauce
2 tortilla wraps
2 handfuls of shredded lettuce
40g grated mozzarella
Chilli flakes to garnish (optional)

💕Start by seasoning the chicken breast all over with salt, pepper, garlic granules and smoked paprika.

💕Next, dip into the egg white, shaking off any excess before dipping into the cornflakes. Press in thoroughly to adhere.

💕Air fry for 17 minutes at 185 degrees celsius, turning part way through. Alternatively, bake at the same temperature for 22-24 minutes.

💕Whilst the chicken cooks, in a bowl, mix together the mayo and chilli puree. Loosen with water if needed.

💕Next, mix together the honey and hot sauce.

💕Once the chicken has cooked, drizzle over the hot honey sauce and slice into strips.

💕Microwave the wraps for 15 seconds before adding the chilli mayo, lettuce, crispy chicken and cheese.

💕Fold into a burrito and pan fry seams down for a minute. If you want to toast the other side you can.

💕Slice into half and garnish with chilli flakes.

Calories per burrito: 496 l Protein: 41.7g l Fat: 10g l Carbs: 56.1g

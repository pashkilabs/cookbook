Texas Twinkies for the Super Bowl 🏈 🐄
Recipe below 👇🏾
��Set @traegergrills to 275*
��Chop up left over brisket to fine shreds
��Use half a block of @phillycreamchs cream cheese
��Shred cheddar cheese to your desire
��Mix all together
��Cut open jalapeño and scoop out seeds
��Fill the jalapeño with brisket and cheese mixture
��Cover the jalapeño with the piece you cut from earlier and wrap in bacon
��Season with your favorite rub
��Put on traeger for 1 hour
��Half way through put on traeger glaze
��Cook for an additional 20-30 mins or until bacon is cooked and jalapeño is cooked to your liking!
��Take em off and let em cool so you don't burn your mouth like me!
��Enjoy 🤙🏾 27w

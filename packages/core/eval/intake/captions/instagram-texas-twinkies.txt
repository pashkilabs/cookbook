Texas Twinkie 🤠🇺🇸
Recipe Below ⤵️⤵️⤵️

Makes 12 ⤵️
🔥12 large jalapeños 
🔥1 block of cream cheese 
🔥1/2 pound chopped Brisket 
🔥12 strips of bacon 🥓 
🔥BBQ rub 

💥Hallow out the Jalapeño like a boat 🛶
💥Add in the cream cheese and brisket 
💥Wrap bacon around the jalapeño making sure to cover the filling 
💥Coat with your favorite bbq rub 
💥Set smoker to 350 degrees 
💥Smoke for 1 hour
💥Then paint on bbq sauce 
💥Back in the smoker for 20 minutes until the sauce gets tacked on 
🤯 Enjoy! 

Pro tip: Place on a sheet pan with an elevated pan grate. This helps so that the bacon grease catches in the pan and you won't get a grease fire in your grill. 👊🏼

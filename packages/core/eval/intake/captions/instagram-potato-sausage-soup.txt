Creamy Potato & Sausage Soup
(I use my instant pot for this but you can use a slow cooker)
Ingredients
• 1 lb Italian sausage (mild or spicy, your choice)
• 6 medium russet or gold potatoes, diced into small cubes
• 1 medium onion, diced
• 3 cloves garlic, minced
• 3 medium carrots, diced
• 3 stalks celery, diced
• 6 cups chicken broth
• 1 ½ tsp salt (adjust to taste)
• ½ tsp black pepper
• 1 tsp dried thyme (or Italian seasoning)
• ½ tsp paprika (optional, for warmth)
• 1 cup heavy cream (or half-and-half for lighter)
• 2 cups chopped kale or spinach
• 2 tbsp flour + 2 tbsp butter (optional, for extra thickness)
Instructions
1. Cook sausage first: Cook sausage until browned, breaking it up into small pieces. Drain excess grease if needed.
2. Add veggies & broth: Add potatoes, onion, garlic, carrots, celery, broth, salt, pepper, thyme, and paprika. Stir well.
3. Slow cook: Put the lid on, set the valve to venting (since it's slow cook mode), and cook on Slow Cook – Normal for 6–7 hours or Slow Cook – High for 3–4 hours, until potatoes are tender.
4. Make it creamy: About 30 minutes before serving, stir in the heavy cream. If you want it thicker, melt butter in a small pan, whisk in flour, then stir this roux into the soup.
5. Add greens: Stir in kale or spinach and let it wilt during the last 5–10 minutes of cook time.
6. Serve: Taste and adjust seasoning if needed. Ladle into bowls and serve warm with crusty bread.

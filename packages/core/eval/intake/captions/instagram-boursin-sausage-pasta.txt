⭐️INGREDIENTS ⭐️
1 lb Rigatoni Pasta
1 lb Italian Sausage (@jimmydean)
1 24oz Jar Tomato Basil Sauce (@carbonefinefood)
5.3 oz @boursincheese with Garlic & Fine Herbs
⭐️ INSTRUCTIONS ⭐️
1️⃣Get a pot of salted water boiling for your pasta. Once boiling, add pasta and cook the pasta al dente as specified time on the box. Once it's done, strain the pasta but reserve a cup of pasta water for later.
2️⃣ Meanwhile, add sausage to a large sauce pan or braiser heated over medium-high heat. Use a spatula or meat masher to break the sausage up into tiny crumbles.
3️⃣ Once the sausage is cooked through, it's time to add the Boursin Cheese and the jar of tomato sauce. Turn the heat down to medium and stir the sauce mixture.
4️⃣ Once melted, add in the al dente pasta and stir to combine. Once all the pasta is coated in the sauce, add half a cup of the reserved pasta water and toss once more.
5️⃣ Taste and season with salt and pepper if needed before serving. Optional, but you can also serve with parmigiano if you wish.
